What We Do

Our Services

We offer a wide range of comprehensive Social Care services. We offer support for both Private and Public family law cases, as well as Child In Need, Child Protection, and Pre-Proceedings.

Parenting Assessments

We complete assessments of adults with learning disabilities and learning difficulties, neurodivergence (including Autism and ADHD), or specific communication needs. Our approach is accessible, fair and focused on each family's individual circumstances.

Our services include:

  • Standard Parenting Assessments
  • Specialist Parenting Assessments (including MALD, Resolutions (denied child abuse) and Parent Assess)
  • Culturally sensitive assessments – assessing those who do not speak English as a first language
  • Capacity to Protect Assessments
  • Risk assessments (including neglect, physical harm, emotional harm, sexual harm, domestic abuse, exploitation, denied contact)
  • Viability and Connected Carer Assessments (Form K)
  • Special Guardianship Order (SGO) Assessments
  • Court reports
  • Section 7
  • Section 37
  • Progression of contact / Family Time
  • Supervision of contact / Family Time

Independent Advocacy Service

We provide independent, person-centred Advocacy to help individuals overcome barriers to communication, understanding and engagement. This includes attendance at meetings, assessment sessions and Court hearings.

The Resolutions Approach

Resolutions-based Parenting Assessments

We offer comprehensive, specialist Resolutions-based parenting assessments, informed by the Resolutions Approach, developed by Andrew Turnell. We are trained in the full Resolutions model, exploring parenting capacity, identified risks, parental insight and acceptance, and the ability of the support network to mitigate risk and promote safe, sustainable caregiving.

Assessments follow the Assessment Triangle and include detailed interviews with network members, network meetings and include robust safety planning.

Each assessment is tailored to the family’s circumstances, providing an evidence-informed, child-focused analysis of risk, protective factors and the potential for safe parenting with appropriate support.

How long does an assessment take?

Every case is different. Assessments usually take 8–12 weeks to complete, but we’ll be happy to talk through timescales and availability.
