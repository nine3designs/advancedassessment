About Advanced Assessment & Solutions

Independent Expert Witness & Social Work Practice dedicated to thorough, objective, and compassionate family court evaluations.

Meet Sara Dawson

Advanced Assessment & Solutions was created by Sara Dawson, a Leading Expert Witness and qualified children's Social Worker with 13 years of complex Child Protection and Court work experience[cite: 1].

Sara began her practice in Rotherham, working directly with families from diverse cultural and socio-economic backgrounds[cite: 1]. Recognising key gaps in cultural advocacy, she became a champion for working with Roma communities, delivering tailored parenting courses to families from Slovakia, Romania, and the Czech Republic alongside Roma Interpreters[cite: 1]. Her team’s outstanding commitment earned them an 'Outstanding' grading from Ofsted[cite: 1].

Throughout her career, Sara has evaluated cases involving parents with learning disabilities and difficulties, as well as complex child protection proceedings regarding neglect and emotional, sexual, and physical harm[cite: 1].

As an Independent Social Worker, she specialises in neurodivergence, domestic abuse, trauma, and complex adult needs, always ensuring that child safety remains paramount while parents are treated with dignity and fairness[cite: 1].
