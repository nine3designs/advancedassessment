Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies are used on this website. Last updated 22 October 2026.

Do we use cookies?

This website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels or any similar third-party trackers.

Essential functionality

The site uses only the basic functionality built into your browser (for example remembering scroll position or form content while you complete it). No persistent cookies are placed on your device by us.

Third-party content

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google receives your request (including your IP address) under its own policies. Links from this site to email, phone, WhatsApp or Google Calendar take you to those services, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Because we set no tracking cookies, doing so will not change how this website works.
