Operational Structure

What We Do

Our Assessment Services

Comprehensive, independent expert witness assessments tailored for family court proceedings and local authority instructions.

01

Neurodivergence & Learning Disabilities

Specialised parenting assessments for adults with cognitive impairments, neurodivergence (Autism, ADHD), learning difficulties, or adapted communication requirements.

  • PAMs & Adapted Assessment Frameworks
  • Cognitive accessibility adjustments
  • Parental capacity evaluation
02

Trauma & Domestic Abuse

In-depth evaluations assessing the impact of domestic abuse, historic trauma, and mental health difficulties on parenting capacity and child safety.

  • Trauma-informed interviewing
  • Risk assessment & safety planning
  • Mental health impact analysis
03

Child Protection & Risk Assessments

Expert witness reports for cases involving complex neglect, emotional, sexual, or physical harm in court care proceedings.

  • Public & Private law expert witness reports
  • Analysis of neglect and risk indicators
  • Child-centred best-interest recommendations
04

Cultural & Language Advocacy

Culturally sensitive assessments working with non-English speaking families, diverse communities, and specialist interpreter services.

  • Roma community specialist insight
  • Interpreter-supported parent evaluations
  • Cross-cultural family assessments
Also instructed for Specialist Family & Parenting Assessments Risk, Safeguarding & Carer Assessments Court Reports, Supervised Contact & Direct Work with Children

Need something more specific?

Every instruction is scoped individually — tell us what the court needs and we will confirm availability and timescales.

About the Practice

About Advanced Assessment & Solutions

Independent Expert Witness & Social Work Practice dedicated to thorough, objective, and compassionate family court evaluations.

Meet Sara Dawson

Advanced Assessment & Solutions was created by Sara Dawson, a Leading Expert Witness and qualified children’s Social Worker with 13 years of complex Child Protection and Court work experience.

Sara began her practice in Rotherham, working directly with families from diverse cultural and socio-economic backgrounds. Recognising key gaps in cultural advocacy, she became a champion for working with Roma communities, delivering tailored parenting courses to families from Slovakia, Romania, and the Czech Republic alongside Roma interpreters. Her team’s outstanding commitment earned them an ‘Outstanding’ grading from Ofsted.

Throughout her career, Sara has evaluated cases involving parents with learning disabilities and difficulties, as well as complex child protection proceedings regarding neglect and emotional, sexual, and physical harm.

As an Independent Social Worker, she specialises in neurodivergence, domestic abuse, trauma, and complex adult needs — always ensuring that child safety remains paramount while parents are treated with dignity and fairness.

Leading Expert Witness
Sara Dawson

Specialist Assessor | Consultant
Founder & Lead Independent Social Work Expert
