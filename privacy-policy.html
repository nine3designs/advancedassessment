Privacy Policy

How Advanced Assessment & Solutions collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Advanced Assessment & Solutions Ltd (“we”, “us”) is an independent social work practice led by Sara Dawson, providing expert assessments, parenting assessments and expert witness services to families, solicitors and the courts. For the purposes of UK data protection law, we are the controller of your personal information.

What information we collect

  • Enquiries: when you contact us through the website, by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the details you provide — typically your name, contact details and the nature of your enquiry.
  • Instructions and assessments: where we are instructed (for example by a solicitor, local authority or the court), case information is shared with us by the instructing parties and handled under our professional and legal obligations.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) for security and performance.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, to carry out assessments and expert witness work we are instructed to provide, to communicate with you, and to keep the records we are professionally required to keep. We never sell personal information, and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

Our lawful basis

We rely on consent (when you contact us voluntarily), legitimate interests (running our practice and responding to enquiries), contract (when we are engaged to provide services) and legal obligation or the administration of justice (court-directed work). Where information about children, health or other special category data is involved, it is processed only where the law permits — for example in connection with family proceedings.

Sharing and storage

Information shared with us as part of an assessment is seen only by those who need it — typically the instructing party and, where directed, the court. We store information securely, keep it only as long as our professional record-keeping standards and the law require, and destroy it securely afterwards.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will always be on this page.
