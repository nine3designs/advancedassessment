Meet Sara

Advanced Assessment & Solutions was created by Sara Dawson in 2021, an experienced Leading Expert Witness and qualified children's Social Worker who qualified in 2021. Sara has significant experience in complex Child Protection cases and Court proceedings.

Sara began her career in Rotherham, supporting families from a wide range of cultural backgrounds. She developed a strong commitment to supporting families from Eastern Europe, delivering parenting programmes with the help of a Roma Interpreter. The team was rated 'Outstanding' by Ofsted.

Sara specialises in assessing adults with learning disabilities, neurodivergence, experiences of domestic abuse and trauma, sexual abuse, protective carers and complex mental health difficulties.

Amazing Independent Social Worker 2024 & 2026

Recognised by the British Association of Social Workers (BASW) as an Amazing Independent Social Worker in both 2024 and 2026, Sara is passionate about making a meaningful difference in children's lives.
