Meet Sara

Advanced Assessment & Solutions was created by Sara Dawson in 2021, an experienced Leading Expert Witness and children's Social Worker who qualified in 2012. Sara has significant experience in complex Child Protection cases and Court proceedings.

Sara is an experienced Social Work practitioner with extensive expertise in complex safeguarding matters, parenting assessments and family reunification. Throughout her career, she has worked across several local authorities in South Yorkshire, supporting families from diverse cultural backgrounds. This experience includes working with Eastern European families, delivering parenting programmes with the support of an Interpreter to ensure culturally sensitive and accessible support. The service Sara worked for was rated Outstanding by Ofsted.

Sara has extensive experience across Child Protection, Care Proceedings, the Public Law Outline (PLO), Special Guardianship and adoption assessments. Sara has worked with families experiencing a range of complex challenges, including neglect, domestic abuse, substance misuse, child exploitation and learning difficulties.

Skilled in evidence-based assessment, risk analysis, safety planning and multi-agency collaboration, Sara is committed to achieving positive, sustainable outcomes for children and their families. She has a child-centred approach, balancing the need to safeguard children with the importance of supporting parents to make meaningful and lasting changes.

BASW Awards

Amazing Independent Social Worker 2024 & 2026

Recognised by the British Association of Social Workers (BASW) as an Amazing Independent Social Worker in both 2024 and 2026, Sara is passionate about making a meaningful difference in children's lives. This recognition reflects her commitment to compassionate, person-centred practice, promoting positive outcomes, and ensuring children’s safety and best interests remain at the heart of her practice.
