Training & Qualifications

Training & Professional Qualifications

Over 13 years of specialised practice, supported by extensive specialist training and continuous professional development in parenting assessments, safeguarding, attachment, neurodiversity, learning disabilities, trauma, domestic abuse and expert witness reporting.

Specialist Training & Professional Development


01 13+ Years Post-Qualifying Experience

Parenting, Attachment & Child Development

Infant Care-Index Accredited Training (9 days) – Specialist training focused on observing and assessing parent–infant interactions, with particular expertise in relationships between parents and babies and toddlers aged 0–15 months.


Attachment Training –
Specialist understanding of attachment, relationships and the impact of early experiences on children and parenting.

Assessing Parental Capacity to Change –
Specialist training in evaluating parental insight, motivation, capacity for change and the implications for children.


Working Together With Parents Network –
Specialist practice in working collaboratively with parents and families within assessment and safeguarding contexts.

Working with Parents with Learning Disabilities and Learning Difficulties and Their Children -
Specialist approaches to assessment and intervention that promote accessible, inclusive and strengths-based practice.

BAAF Connected Carer (Form K) –
Specialist training in undertaking Connected Carer and viability assessments, including the assessment of family and connected carers within care proceedings.
02 Assessment Related

Learning Disabilities, Neurodiversity & Accessible Assessment

MALD (Making Assessments Accessible for Parents with Learning Disabilities and Learning Difficulties and Other Additional Needs) -
Specialist training in adapting assessment approaches to ensure parents can meaningfully participate and demonstrate their parenting capacity.

Parent Assess (2016) –
Specialist assessment framework designed for parents with learning disabilities and learning difficulties, incorporating visual aids and accessible assessment tools.

Working with Neurodivergent Parents in the Court Arena -
Specialist training in understanding and supporting neurodivergent parents within care proceedings and the family court environment.

Understanding Autism, Dyslexia & ADHD –
Training focused on recognising and responding to the needs of neurodivergent children and adults.

PAMS 4.0 Training & Licensed Practitioner –
Accredited use of the Parenting Assessment Manual Software (PAMS 4.0) for assessing parents with learning disabilities and cognitive difficulties.

CUBAS –
Specialist training in the use of CUBAS within assessment and safeguarding practice.
03 Safeguarding

Safeguarding, Risk & Harm

Sex Offender Training –
Specialist training in understanding sexual offending, risk factors and safeguarding considerations.

Inflicted Injuries in Care Proceedings –
Specialist training in understanding and assessing cases involving inflicted injury within care proceedings.

Non-Fatal Strangulation –
Specialist training in recognising, understanding and assessing the risks and impacts associated with non-fatal strangulation.

Sexually Harmful Behaviour (2 days) – Lucy Faithfull Foundation –
Specialist training focused on understanding and responding to harmful sexual behaviour in young people with autism.

Denied Child Abuse – Resolutions Model, delivered by Andrew Turnell –
Specialist training in the Resolutions approach to working with families where allegations or concerns regarding child abuse are disputed or denied.

04 Domestic Abuse

Domestic Abuse & Safeguarding Practice

Safe & Together –
Specialist domestic abuse training focused on supporting the child to remain safely cared for by the non-abusive parent, while recognising the impact of domestic abuse on children and parenting.

Signs of Safety – Enhanced Training (7 days) –
Advanced training in strengths-based safeguarding, risk assessment and collaborative child protection practice.

Restorative Practice –
Training in restorative approaches to relationships, conflict, accountability and collaborative family work.

Trauma-Informed Practice –
Specialist training in understanding the impact of trauma and applying trauma-informed approaches to assessment and intervention.

04 Continuous Professional Development

CPD & Specialist Methodologies

  • Cognitive & Learning Disability Assessment Methodologies
  • Trauma-Informed Practice & Domestic Violence Frameworks
  • Courtroom Skills & Cross-Examination Training for Expert Witnesses
